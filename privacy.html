Privacy Policy

ESIRAK Pin Scheduler · Last updated: November 15, 2026

ESIRAK Pin Scheduler is a private tool operated by a single person (ESIRAK) for scheduling and publishing that person's own pins to their own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public and is not used by other people.

What the app does

The app lets its owner prepare a pin (image, title, description, destination link, board and publish time) through a Telegram bot. At the chosen time, the app creates the pin on the owner's Pinterest board using the Pinterest API.

Data we access and store

  • Pinterest authorization token of the app owner, obtained through Pinterest's official OAuth flow. It is stored as an encrypted secret on the hosting platform (Cloudflare) and is used only to create pins and read board names.
  • Pin queue: the images, titles, descriptions, links, board choices and publish times the owner enters, kept until the pin is published or removed.
  • Telegram user ID of the owner, used so that only the owner can control the bot.

The app requests only the permissions it needs: reading boards and creating pins.

Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other Pinterest or Telegram users. It does not ask for Pinterest passwords, does not use session cookies, and does not use any login method other than OAuth.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties. It is processed only by the services needed to run the app: Pinterest (to publish pins), Telegram (to receive the owner's commands) and Cloudflare and GitHub (hosting and file storage).

Retention and deletion

Queued pin data is removed after publishing or when the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest account settings, and can delete all stored data by removing the app's secrets and queue from the hosting platform.
